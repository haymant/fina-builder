# Narrative Intelligence — one cue per recorded demo beat.
# Keep each line in beat order. The exporter uses demo-timeline.json.
Narrative Intelligence runs inside fina-builder. Open the Narrative menu and choose the workspace. This demo uses a synthetic feed and a local ledger.
The desktop workspace is ready.
The workspace opens on the local NIP ledger, with no cloud service in the path.
Five gates: service, provider, source, transcript, and evidence-linked claims.
Rights confirmation comes before any fetch. One approval subscribes the feed and starts the sync.
RSS discovery finds the episode. Only metadata arrives until you transcribe.
Transcribe locally. Long audio is split into five-minute chunks, and the job line shows each step through commit.
The retained event is immutable: source, publication, capture and ingestion times, plus a content hash.
Remote extraction stays blocked until consent is granted for this provider and model.
Once granted, extraction may send text to that provider. Revocation is audited.
Extraction commits claims with evidence quotes and a full derivation record.
Claim Explorer shows confidence, provider, model, prompt version, and every linked source quote.
Clusters and the graph are versioned projections. Every edge points back to retained evidence.
Follow an edge and the graph opens the immutable origin event, not a copy.
Radar compares completed UTC weeks and labels coverage honestly. Timeline keeps the same point-in-time evidence and never invents a score.
ETF mappings are dated research records. Editorial candidates stay unreviewed until a person decides.
Cached media and the per-step job log stay reviewable, so you can replay the exact downloaded audio.
That is the journey: rights, discovery, local transcription, retained evidence, and derived views you can verify.
